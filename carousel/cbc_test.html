CBC — gem tests

The gem is thirteen identical circles. One at the centre, eight on a lattice a circle-and-a-gutter out, four more further out on the axes — each clipped to its own cell in a five-by-five grid. Measured off your reference: the outer radius is exactly 2.79 circles, which is also the ratio that lets the whole mark nest inside its own centre circle. That is what makes an endless inward flow possible without the mark ever running out of itself.

Tonal by default. The button flips to flat red on white to check the shapes.
