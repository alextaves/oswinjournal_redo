Grafting — rope tests

A rope is fibres twisted into yarns and yarns into strands, each level wound against the one below it — which is the whole trick, since it's the opposing twists that stop it unravelling. These are built the same way: loose fibres hang slack down the card, and a laying front rises from the bottom, pulling everything below it into one closed rope as it climbs. Four takes on how fine the fibres are and how hard the twist is.
